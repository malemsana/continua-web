About Continua

Continua is an independent educational platform/project focused on making study materials easier to discover, access, organize, and use. We bring together structured resources for school study — in one calm, readable place — so students can spend less time searching and more time learning.

About Continua

Continua exists to make everyday study simpler. Instead of scattered PDFs, screenshots, and forwarded files, it offers a clear, chapter-based library where materials are easy to find and pleasant to read.

The approach is straightforward: organize content by class, subject, and chapter; present it with consistent typography and careful structure; and keep the reading experience focused on learning rather than decoration.

Across the library you will find resources such as:

  • Structured study materials
  • Chapter-based organization
  • NCERT resources where applicable
  • Solutions and worked examples
  • Formulae and revision material
  • Past Years Questions
  • Mock Tests & Sample Papers
  • Reference materials
  • Other educational resources

Continua is an independent educational platform/project — not presented as a legally incorporated company — built as a small, established educational library. What matters is legibility, accuracy, and ease of use for students, teachers, and families.

How Continua handles content

Resources available through Continua may come from different sources. In general, the platform may provide:

  • Original Continua content
  • Third-party educational material made available under its own terms
  • NCERT E-content under the applicable NCERT licence
  • Links or references to external resources

Continua does not claim ownership of third-party material. Ownership, attribution, and permitted use for each resource are determined by its own licence, not by its presence on Continua.

For the relevant terms, see the Continua Free Educational License for original Continua content, the NCERT E-Content Arrangement for NCERT material, and our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for the overall service.
