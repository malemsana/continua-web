NCERT E-Content Arrangement

How NCERT material is made available through Continua

Continua provides access to selected NCERT E-content — such as textbook chapters made available by the National Council of Educational Research and Training — so that students can read and study permitted material directly on the platform alongside Continua's own resources.

NCERT E-content is not owned by Continua and is not covered by the Continua Free Educational License (CFEL). It remains the property of NCERT and is governed solely by the NCERT Copyright License reproduced below. Continua's role is limited to making permitted NCERT material accessible in accordance with that licence, without claiming ownership or granting additional rights over it.

At a glance
  • Owner: NCERT is the corporate author and sole owner of NCERT E-content and is entitled to enforce its rights.
  • Not Continua-owned: Continua does not claim ownership of NCERT E-content.
  • Governing licence: NCERT E-content is governed by NCERT's own licence, not by CFEL.
  • Not covered by CFEL: Nothing in CFEL transfers ownership of NCERT material or modifies rights granted by NCERT.
  • Continua's role: To make permitted NCERT material accessible through the platform under the terms NCERT allows.
Using NCERT material: Before downloading, sharing, or distributing any NCERT E-content, please read the NCERT licence below. It explains what you may do — including reading online, downloading freely, and non-commercial distribution with attribution — and what is not permitted.
